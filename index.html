About Trinton Dailey

Experience You Can Trust

Hi, I'm Trinton Dailey. I grew up in Pine River, Minnesota, where my dad introduced me to the woods at a young age. When I was 10 years old, he got me started in forestry and logging, and it quickly became a passion that has stayed with me ever since. By the time I was 16, I was working full-time in the woods, building the hands-on experience that has shaped my career.

More than 20 years later, I'm still doing what I love. Forestry isn't just my profession—it's a way of life. Even when I'm spending time with my family or on vacation, you'll usually find us in the woods hiking, exploring, hunting, or simply enjoying the outdoors. My passion for healthy forests and responsible land stewardship doesn't end when the workday is over.

Over the years, I've gained hands-on experience in timber harvesting, forest management, wildlife habitat improvement, and helping landowners make informed decisions about their property. I believe every woodland is unique, and every recommendation should be tailored to the landowner's goals while protecting the long-term health of the forest.

At Dailey Forest Management & Consulting, my goal is simple: to provide honest advice, professional service, and practical solutions that help landowners get the most from their woods while caring for them for future generations.

Whether you're planning a timber sale, improving wildlife habitat, or creating a long-term forest management plan, I'd be honored to help you achieve your goals.
